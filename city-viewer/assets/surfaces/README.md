# Photographed surfaces and reflections

The bundled Poly Haven textures come from real material surfaces. Albedo JPEGs use sRGB. OpenGL normal maps use linear sampling. Albedo tiles are 512 or 1024 pixels and normal tiles are 512 pixels. Their UVs follow the source capture dimensions. The 1K HDR image supplies daytime reflections for car paint, glass and trim. The game's sky supplies the reflection at night. ## Rain jacket fabric

`ripstop-color.jpg` is a generated 512-pixel albedo tile for the player and crowd jackets. It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool and compressed to JPEG for the game.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square tileable colour/albedo texture for a 3D London game character's technical rain jacket, to be tinted by the jacket material. True-to-life macro material photograph of a flat neutral light grey nylon ripstop swatch. Fill the entire frame edge to edge with uninterrupted fabric. Perfectly orthographic, uniform diffuse cross-polarised illumination, microscopically fine warp and weft with a very faint regular ripstop grid. It should look like tightly woven outdoor clothing, matte and subtle. Seamless horizontal and vertical repetition with matching phase at the edges. No gradient, highlights, shadows, wrinkles, seams, border, labels, writing, stitches or objects. Square composition.
